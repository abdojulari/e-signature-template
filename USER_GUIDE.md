# 📖 Signature Generator Pro - Complete User Guide

## 🌟 Welcome to Signature Generator Pro!

**Signature Generator Pro** is a powerful Chrome extension that helps you create beautiful, professional email signatures and automatically apply them to your Gmail and Yahoo emails. This comprehensive guide will walk you through everything you need to know.

---

## 🔗 Installation Guide

### Step 1: Visit the Chrome Web Store

1. **Open Google Chrome** on your computer
2. **Navigate to the Chrome Web Store** by clicking this link:
   
   👉 **[Install Signature Generator Pro](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/signature-generator-pro/pjmcfpndnehpblkgckmjmmapejfjoeic?authuser=0&hl=en)**

3. You'll see the extension page with details about Signature Generator Pro

### Step 2: Install the Extension

1. **Click the "Add to Chrome" button** (blue button in the top right)
2. A popup will appear asking for permissions
3. **Click "Add extension"** to confirm the installation
4. You'll see a confirmation message: "Signature Generator Pro has been added to Chrome"

### Step 3: Pin the Extension (Recommended)

1. Look for the **puzzle piece icon** (🧩) in your Chrome toolbar
2. **Click the puzzle piece** to see your extensions
3. **Find "Signature Generator Pro"** in the list
4. **Click the pin icon** (📌) next to it to pin it to your toolbar
5. Now you'll see the Signature Generator Pro icon directly in your toolbar for easy access

## 🔧 Troubleshooting Common Issues

### Extension Not Working

**Problem**: Extension icon is grayed out or not responding
**Solutions**:
1. **Refresh the webpage** you're trying to use it on
2. **Check if you're on a supported email site** (Gmail or Yahoo)
3. **Restart Chrome** and try again
4. **Reload the extension**: Go to `chrome://extensions/`, find Signature Generator Pro, and click the reload icon

### Signature Not Applying

**Problem**: Clicking "Apply Signature" doesn't insert anything
**Solutions**:
1. **Make sure you're in compose mode** (writing a new email or reply)
2. **Click inside the email compose area** before applying signature
3. **Try refreshing the page** and composing a new email
4. **Check if JavaScript is enabled** in your browser

### Signature Looks Different in Email

**Problem**: Signature appears differently than in the preview
**Solutions**:
1. **Different email clients** render HTML differently
2. **Use simpler formatting** for better compatibility
3. **Test with plain text** versions if needed
4. **Check image links** are publicly accessible

### Images Not Loading

**Problem**: Profile photo or company logo not appearing
**Solutions**:
1. **Ensure images are under 500KB** in size
2. **Use common formats**: JPG, PNG, or SVG
3. **Check internet connection** when uploading
4. **Try different images** to isolate the issue

## 🔒 Privacy & Security

### Your Data is Safe
- **Local Storage**: Signatures are stored securely in your browser
- **No Cloud Storage**: We don't store your personal information on our servers
- **OAuth Security**: Authentication is handled by Google/Microsoft directly
- **No Tracking**: We don't track your email usage or content

### Permissions Explained
- **activeTab**: Allows the extension to work on the current tab (Gmail/Yahoo)
- **storage**: Saves your signature preferences locally
- **scripting**: Enables signature insertion into email compose areas
- **host_permissions**: Access to Gmail and Yahoo domains only
